# The Scene I Can't Stop Thinking About

There's a scene in a film where a character finally says the thing they've been carrying for the whole movie, and the camera doesn't move. No close-up. No music. Just the room, the two of them, the sentence landing like a stone in water.

I've been rewriting that scene in my head with different characters ever since. It's the only scene I know how to write.

— night five
